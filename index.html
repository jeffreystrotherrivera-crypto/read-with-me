How Read With Me thinks 🧠

Every answer wears a badge that tells you where it was made. Nothing is hidden.

  • 📱 ON-DEVICE · Book Brain

    Answers built into the app: our kid dictionary and facts about each page. Works in airplane mode. Nothing leaves the device.

  • 📱 ON-DEVICE · Gemini Nano

    If Chrome on this computer has its built-in AI model (Gemini Nano, through Chrome’s Prompt API), new questions can be answered on the device too. Still nothing leaves the device.

  • 📱 ON-DEVICE · Firebase AI Logic

    Firebase AI Logic in hybrid mode asked for an answer and Gemini Nano on this device made it.

  • ☁️ CLOUD · Firebase AI Logic

    For the big asks — a brand-new ending, real-world facts — Firebase AI Logic sends the question to a Gemini model in the cloud. Grown-ups can turn the cloud off in the Grown-ups panel.

Try it: turn on airplane mode and tap a word — it still works. Then turn the internet back on and tap “Make me a new ending.”

On-device AI through Firebase AI Logic needs Chrome on desktop (139+) and the Firebase JS SDK v12.5.0+. Read With Me also talks to Chrome’s Prompt API directly for its Gemini Nano layer. The built-in Book Brain layer works in every browser, online or off.

Your child’s privacy

  • 🔒 Questions stay on this device whenever they can be answered here.
  • 🗒️ Questions are never saved. They live only in this page’s memory while it is open — close the tab and they are gone.
  • ▶️ The one thing this device remembers is where the read-aloud stopped on each page, so “Read to me” can pick the story up in the same place. It never leaves the device.
  • 🚫 No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • ☁️ The cloud is used only for “big asks” — and only if you leave it on, above.
